# CapChief privacy policy

CapChief reads your screen only when you take a capture or record a GIF. What it captures stays on
your PC, and it never collects or sends personal data.

- **No network.** CapChief never connects to the internet. It has no accounts, ads, telemetry or
  update checks.
- **Your captures stay on your PC.** A capture is saved only where you save it, and copied only
  when you copy it. The history of your last captures is kept in memory and is gone when CapChief
  quits.
- **GIF recordings.** While a recording is in the history, CapChief keeps it in a temporary file in
  your Windows temp folder, and deletes it when it is no longer needed.
- **Error log.** If something goes wrong, CapChief writes the error to a log file on your PC, in
  its folder under your local app data. The log is never sent anywhere.

When you install or update CapChief from the Microsoft Store, Microsoft may collect data about
that, and Windows may send crash reports to Microsoft, depending on your Windows settings. That is
covered by Microsoft's privacy statement, not by CapChief.
